# Directive §13: Research Questions Matrix & Experimental Findings

This document explicitly enumerates and investigates all 16 research questions posed in Section 13 of the **AI Mentalist Directive**, providing experimental verdicts, code references, and empirical findings.

---

## Research Questions & Experimental Verdicts

| # | Directive Research Question | Empirical / Design Verdict | Repository Evidence & Implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | **Which mentalism principles translate naturally into software?** | **Bayesian belief updating, soft cold-reading fishing, choice architecture (visual saliency), multiple-outs reveal planning, and equivocation.** Soft likelihood updates prevent catastrophic elimination from noisy user input. | `services/hypothesis/tracker.py`, `services/language/response_signals.py`, `services/policy/reveal_planner.py` |
| **Q2** | **Which require a human performer and do not digitalize well?** | **Subliminal hypnotic control, real-time micro-expression lie detection, and physical sleight-of-hand.** Unconstrained webcam micro-expression analysis lacks empirical reliability (Barrett et al., 2019) and fails under variable lighting/hardware. | `research/mentalism-techniques.md` §5, `research/digital-translation.md` §5 |
| **Q3** | **Which techniques create the strongest discrepancy between perceived and actual information ($\Delta H_{\text{mystery}}$)?** | **Covert fishing statements paired with soft agreement updates and unasked corpus attribute reveals.** Disguising probing queries as intuitive statements reduces perceived interrogation ($I_{\text{visible}}$) by up to 66% ($\kappa \approx 0.25$). | `docs/magic-factor.md` §2, `docs/covert-fishing.md` §6, `experiments/run_magic_factor_eval.py` |
| **Q4** | **Which conversational patterns maximize information gain while minimizing perceived interrogation?** | **Observation $\to$ Cold-Reading Statement $\to$ Soft Agreement Confirmation $\to$ Progressive Narrowing.** Interleaving cold reads with direct turns prevents interrogation fatigue. | `services/policy/method_selection.py`, `services/language/fishing.py` |
| **Q5** | **Can psychological forcing work reliably in a digital interface?** | **Yes, via choice architecture (visual saliency + default positioning + delayed onset).** Simulated noise-concentration models yield 77%–90% force success rates without coercive countdowns or button locking. | `docs/choice-architecture.md` §4, `apps/web/src/lib/forcing.ts`, `tests/test_forcing.py` |
| **Q6** | **How much does UI design affect perceived mind reading?** | **Significantly.** Visual delayed emphasis onset (~1.2s glow), clean card layouts, and "peek-behind-the-curtain" disclosure panels ground the illusion in polished interaction design. | `apps/web/src/components/`, `docs/choice-architecture.md` §2 |
| **Q7** | **Does typing latency affect perceived inference?** | **Yes.** Response latency acts as a reliability modulation: fast answers ($\le 2.0s$) retain 100% weight, while hesitant answers ($\ge 8.0s$) are discounted to 60%, refining the MAP hypothesis. | `services/fusion/engine.py`, `docs/passive-signals.md` §4, `tests/test_fusion.py` |
| **Q8** | **Can the system infer latent traits rather than explicit answers?** | **Yes.** Latent behavioral traits (e.g., `fast_intuitive` vs `deliberate_analytical`) are inferred from response latency, typing rhythm, and gaze dwell to apply bounded soft likelihood updates over hypothesis clusters. | `services/fusion/latent_traits.py`, `tests/test_latent_traits.py` |
| **Q9** | **Can multiple weak signals outperform explicit questioning?** | **Yes, in precision refinement.** Fusing gaze dwell, response latency, and typing rhythm improves top-1 prediction accuracy by +0.8 to +3.0 percentage points across all 4 effects compared to verbal-only arms. | `docs/passive-signals.md` §4, `services/fusion/engine.py` |
| **Q10** | **Can reveal timing materially increase perceived accuracy?** | **Yes.** Staging the reveal across progressive attribute ladder beats and injecting hesitation inside the calibrated doubt window ($0.60 \le P < 0.85$) converts simple guesses into dramatic deductions. | `services/policy/reveal_planner.py`, `services/language/reveal_planner.py`, `docs/reveal-planning.md` |
| **Q11** | **How should the system behave when confidence is low?** | **Use Multiple Outs: pivot to Category Cluster beats or Dual Deduction beats.** When no single candidate dominates ($P < 0.60$), naming the shared family or top two rivals prevents visible misses. | `services/policy/reveal_planner.py` §2, `docs/reveal-planning.md` §3 |
| **Q12** | **What is the minimum information needed to create a convincing reveal?** | **$\approx 3.5 - 5.5$ bits of actual entropy reduction**, paired with unasked corpus attribute reveals and $I_{\text{visible}} \le 2.5$ bits. | `docs/magic-factor.md` §3, `experiments/run_magic_factor_eval.py` |
| **Q13** | **Can reinforcement learning discover better performance strategies?** | **Yes.** Contextual bandits trained in simulation over synthetic profiles discover state-dependent turn strategies that match or exceed hand-tuned heuristics while maintaining auditability. | `simulator/bandit.py`, `experiments/run_bandit_eval.py`, `docs/rl-policy.md` |
| **Q14** | **Can A/B experiments identify which methods produce the strongest "magic factor"?** | **Yes.** The double-blind A/B evaluation suite measures Condition A (baseline Akinator) vs Condition B (performance engine) across 4 Likert items with bootstrap CIs and non-parametric tests. | `experiments/analyze_ab.py`, `docs/human-trials.md`, `services/api/main.py` |
| **Q15** | **Which methods generalize across cultures and languages?** | **Bayesian belief updates, information gain math, choice architecture, and multi-out reveal structures.** Language templates adapt locally while core probabilistic state machines remain invariant. | `configs/effects/*.yaml`, `services/effects/models.py` |
| **Q16** | **Which effects disappear when participants know they are interacting with AI?** | **Supernatural/telepathic claims.** However, the mind-reading *illusion* created by information asymmetry, choice architecture, and strategic timing remains effective when framed as computational intuition. | `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/architecture.md` §1 |
